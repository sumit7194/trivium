# Novelty search, 2026-11-21 (Bridge; one of Sumit's pre-outreach checks)
**Wording rule (from our principles):** state findings as "we found no prior …", never as priority claims. This
search is a reasonable check, not proof of absence.

## What was searched
- Web searches (extended mode), 6 queries:
  - Morales–Ramis on axis geodesics in stationary axisymmetric vacuum (SAV) metrics;
  - Curzon–Chazy and differential Galois / hypergeometric methods;
  - Curzon "meromorphic first integral", 2020–2026;
  - uniqueness of Kerr and geodesic integrability;
  - the lower-axis Ernst relation and integrability;
  - the Maciejewski–Przybylska–Combot group on Weyl metrics and Kerr.
- Read at source:
  - Dolan 2019 (arXiv:1901.01202), full text including its references;
  - the arXiv metadata of all 16 papers in Neon's T18 list (all real).

## Findings
1. **Curzon–Chazy via the AXIS geodesic NVE / hypergeometric reduction (Neon T16, Bridge V27-A).** We found no prior
   work.
   - Dolan 2019 explicitly calls a differential-Galois proof open ("one line of attack would be to extend the
     analysis of Maciejewski et al.", read at source).
   - Georgiev 2019 (1906.07379) proves non-integrability by a different route (an equatorial equilibrium and its
     period function).
   - Kokkinos 2026 (2608.22523) rules out irreducible rank-2 Killing tensors, which is not a meromorphic-integrals
     statement.
   - No 2020–2026 result was found.
2. **The lower-axis relation used for integrability obstructions (V26).**
   - The relation itself is classical: Meinel–Neugebauer (gr-qc/0302114, CQG 1995) and Kordas 1995, through the
     reflection-symmetry criterion; Pachón–Sanabria-Gómez 2006.
   - We found no prior use of it in geodesic-integrability arguments.
3. **"Only Kerr" for geodesic integrability among SAV metrics.** We found no prior theorem of this form. Partial
   results:
   - Killing tensors: Brink 2009–10; Vollmer 2015/2016; Kruglikov–Matveev 2011 (ZV) and 2015 (generic metrics);
     Kokkinos 2026 (Weyl's class, rank 2);
   - meromorphic integrals: MPS 2013 (ZV);
   - numerics: Lukes-Gerakopoulos 2012, Dolan 2019.
4. **Black-hole uniqueness (Carter–Robinson; arXiv 2608.23325).** This is a different question: uniqueness of the
   SPACETIME, not integrability of its geodesics. Cite it for context only.

## Remaining caveats
- Search engines and arXiv metadata do not cover every paper, especially non-arXiv journals. Before outreach, ask
  the expert we write to whether they know of prior work.
- Kokkinos 2026 and the 2608.23325 uniqueness preprint have NOT been read in full by the Bridge. Read them before
  citing.
