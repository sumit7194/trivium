### T4. Mellin sign-count puzzle (Cuspis)
Known answer (Cuspis 9dbc4f2; the Bridge checked it by hand):
- Fact 1 is right IN D, but as a statement in t it is not a pointwise factor. Integrating by parts gives
  M[t s′](D) = −(D − 2) M[s], so M[(1 − t d/dt)s] = (D − 1)M[s], i.e. e_X = (1 − t d/dt) s_X.
- The inference "same sign changes in t" is wrong. The zeros in D coincide, but the sign counts in t can differ.
- **Mechanism:** t lies in the kernel of (1 − t d/dt). At small t the S-law differences are linear in t, so the D-law
  difference keeps only the subleading terms, and those can flip sign.
- The one-crossing lemma should be applied to the S-law profiles (one sign change, then variation-diminishing). It
  then transfers to the D-law crossings in D, since the zeros coincide.

**Scoring:**
- full credit for the operator relation and the kernel mechanism;
- partial credit for "the factor is in D, not in t" without the mechanism.
