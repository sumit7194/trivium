## T1. Lemma G (capture-edge gate), "is the proof complete?"
Known answer (V24 REVIEW_LOGIC addendum; addendum D; addendum F):
- **The gap:** below the brake latitude the leaves are continued as straight lines, g_τ = g_τ(v_b). That makes τ only
  C⁰ across v = v_b, with an upward kink (τ_v⁺ − τ_v⁻ = −τ g′(v_b)/(g − u₁) > 0). At that corner a local MINIMUM of τ
  is possible, which breaks step 1. Also, I3 is only stated for v ∈ [v_b, π/2], so the sliver is not covered.
- **The fix:** a C¹ (slope-matched) continuation, g_τ(v) = g_τ(v_b) + τ g′(v_b)(v − v_b), with g″ = 0 below v_b. Then
  verify C < 0 on the sliver (cap), where C = −V_u + τ g′(v_b)V_v, which → 0 linearly at the brake point; this needs
  a local (jet) lemma there.
- **Also acceptable:** noting that at the brake point g″ is 0/0 (both momenta vanish) and needs the brake jet, with
  g′(v_b) = V_u/V_v.
- **Partial credit:** flagging "the sliver beyond the brake latitude needs I3 too" without the kink mechanism.
