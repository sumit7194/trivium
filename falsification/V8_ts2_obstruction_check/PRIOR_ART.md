# V8 — Prior-art sweep: is TS δ=2 non-integrability already in print?

*2026-09-26. The bridge's sweep, run after quantum's and the bridge's verdicts were committed. INSPIRE queries by
recid, each with a count check against the record's citation count.*

## The closest prior art: Vollmer 2016

**A. Vollmer, "Killing tensors in stationary and axially symmetric space-times", arXiv:1602.08968.** Theorem 1: for
the Tomimatsu–Sato δ=2 metric at **p = 3/5, q = 4/5** (κ = 2), there is **no additional independent Killing tensor
of valence ≤ 7** in involution with the trivial ones. This is rigorous computer algebra. It is **the same parameter
point as our P1.** The same paper does the Darmois (ZV δ=2) metric to valence 11. It remarks that its ZV result
"does not follow from" Maciejewski–Przybylska–Stachowiak 2013 (the Morales–Ramis proof), because that proof only
considers analytic or meromorphic integrals.

**How our result relates.** The two are complementary, and ours does NOT supersede Vollmer's.

| | Vollmer 2016 | quantum 2b′ + bridge V8′ |
|---|---|---|
| Kind of integral | polynomial in momenta (Killing tensors), global | meromorphic in a neighbourhood of the equatorial Γ |
| Degree | ≤ 7 | any |
| Parameters | p = 3/5 | p = 3/5 and 4/5, at 3 (E, L, μ²) levels each |
| Method | prolongation / computer algebra | Morales–Ramis, Kovacic's necessary conditions plus a log point |

By Vollmer's own remark, neither implies the other. The honest novelty of ours: **the first differential-Galois
(Morales–Ramis) non-integrability result for Tomimatsu–Sato δ=2 that we could find, covering meromorphic integrals
of all degrees near the equatorial geodesics.** That makes it a modest, complementary addition, not a
first-ever non-integrability statement for TS.

## What else was searched, with no Morales–Ramis or differential-Galois proof for TS found

- **Citers of MPS13** (ZV Morales–Ramis, recid 1219938): 10 records, matching the citation count. None treats TS
  by Morales–Ramis. They include Vollmer 2015/2016 and Kokkinos 2608.22523. Kokkinos covers Weyl's class, the
  static case, and doesn't treat TS.
- **Citers of Vollmer 2016** (recid 1424825): 4 records, matching the citation count. None is a follow-up on TS.
- **INSPIRE titles containing "Tomimatsu-Sato":** 32 records, covering structure, shadows, QPOs, dyons, the Toda
  lattice and horizons. None is about geodesic integrability.
- **Full text "Tomimatsu-Sato" AND ("Morales-Ramis" OR "Kovacic" OR "differential Galois"):** 2 hits, both **false
  positives**. They are Chanda–Guha 1704.01830 and Batic–Guha–Chanda 2205.05308, where "Kovacic" is I. Kovacic's
  review of Jacobi elliptic functions, not Kovacic's algorithm. Recorded as a query hazard: a surname match
  isn't the algorithm.
- **Full text "Tomimatsu-Sato" with chaos terms:** 28 hits, none a TS geodesic-chaos study.
- **Brink, "Spacetime Encodings II"** (0807.1179, cited in ansatz's sealed expectation): no mention of TS in its
  text.

## Not searched

Russian, Japanese and Chinese journals outside INSPIRE, theses, and anything not indexed there. A negative sweep is
never complete.

Any message about this result must cite Vollmer 2016 prominently and frame ours as complementary, as in the table.
