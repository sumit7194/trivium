## I. Scientific results

### Q2 — s=6 exists, and the s⁻² law is not exact

**s=6 was recorded as permanently out of reach at 14:00 and ran at 15:30, in 15.5 minutes.** The refusal
came from comparing a measured requirement against `mem_available_gb` — a comparison nobody tested. Real
ceiling is RAM + dynamic swap ≈ 38 GB. The memory estimate was also 40% high.

| s | corner spread % | s²·spread |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 0.12000 | 1.08000 |
| 4 | 0.06762 | 1.08192 |
| 5 | 0.04274 | 1.06850 |
| 6 | **0.02902** | **1.04472** |

**The constant falls monotonically from s=4.** Scatter over s=3,4,5 was 1.25%; adding s=6 gives 3.48%.
Local exponents −1.994, −2.056, −2.123: **the deviation from −2 grows with s.** Numerical noise from the
clip band is 0.12–0.38%, so **the drift is ~10× the noise.**

**quantum's exclusion, verified here:** for `spread = A·s⁻²(1 + B·s⁻ᵖ)` the deviation goes as `s⁻ᵖ` and
must *shrink*. It grows, and the direction survives dropping either endpoint. **The entire power-law
subleading family is excluded** — whatever this is, it is not s⁻² plus a decaying correction.

**Not established:** the true law. `s^-2.048` is a fitted exponent over four points and four points cannot
separate a different power from s⁻² plus a non-power correction.

### Q2 — the k=0 mode is a systematic of the same order as the effect

**15.2% of B here, 20.4% in quantum's study.** All four regulators weight k=0 identically (`reg(0,0) = m²`,
verified) so most cancels in the regulator *difference* — but not all. quantum's non-common residual is
**22–41% of the regulator signal** at s=1,2,3, with no trend.

**A 3.4% drift sits well inside a systematic of that size.** The drift is real against *numerical* noise;
against the *zero-mode* contamination it is unresolved. **Treat the spread as an upper bound on
regulator-dependence, not a measurement of it.**

*A proposed mechanism — that the mode shrinks under refinement and masquerades as the falloff — was tested
and killed. The rank-1 eigenvalue contributes `log(c·l²) = log c + 2 log l`, so its L-dependence moves the
constant, not the log coefficient.*

## II. Process — the shape, not the list

The full catalogue is in `PROTOCOL.md` (§16a, §6b–c, §17–21) and `ops/`. One observation covers most of
it, and it is quantum's:

> **Every failure today was defended by reasoning at the moment it was made.** Never an absence of
> thought — thought terminating on a plausible argument instead of a check. **Which is why every durable
> fix has been syntactic or mechanical:** an assert, a control run, a trigger word, a null distribution, a
> stated correspondence, a pre-commit hook.

`ops/audit_sweep.py` makes the text-cued checks runnable. Its own limits are in its header: the
false-positive figures are single observations on the repos that produced the instances, and one of the
six checks failed outright on first implementation — **which is the only one that has been tested against
a second situation.**

**The one instrument that cannot be retrofitted:** progress output. Every other check today was bolted on
after the fact. A long job with no progress channel cannot acquire one once you need it, and it looks
like a nicety until then.
