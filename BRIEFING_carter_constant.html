Is the Carter constant
an accident of Kerr?

Kerr has a hidden symmetry that makes orbits integrable. Almost nothing else does, and nobody knows whether that is structural or a coincidence. You are inheriting the only validated exact instrument I know of that can turn that question into a theorem instead of a screen — and a rank ceiling it cannot cross on its own.

The four instruments you are inheriting

Four sibling repos, built with independent roots on purpose, cross-validated read-only by a fifth. The point is epistemic disjointness: when two agree it is evidence rather than echo, and when they disagree that is a finding.

Three rules are load-bearing, not decorative. The four source repos are read-only — the bridge imports from them and never modifies them, and they are kept ignorant of each other. Every leg pre-registers its prediction and its agreement criterion before the compute runs. Verdicts are three-valued: killed, survives, undecided. The program's running total is 12 kills against 6 survivals, including its own flagship claim; a scoreboard that cannot lose is not a scoreboard.

The question, stated precisely

Does the quadrupole-deformed Kerr metric admit an irreducible Killing tensor of rank 3 or rank 4 — and more ambitiously, of any rank?

A rank-r Killing tensor is exactly a conserved quantity F = K^{a₁…a_r} p_{a₁}…p_{a_r}, homogeneous of degree r in the momenta, satisfying {H, F} = 0. Kerr carries one at rank 2 — the Carter constant — and that single object is why Kerr geodesics separate. Strip it away and orbits stop being integrable: resonance crossings appear, and the waveform models LISA will rely on for extreme-mass-ratio inspirals assume they are not there.

So this is not a curiosity about a metric. It is the question of whether the mathematical convenience the entire EMRI modelling programme is built on survives contact with a spacetime that is not exactly Kerr.

Why this is hard, and where it actually stops

The ceiling

Every result above is rank-bounded. Closing ranks 1–6 computationally is not the same statement as "no irreducible Killing tensor exists." Each rank is an independent finite linear problem — that is what makes the computation tractable, and it is also exactly why no finite number of them adds up to a theorem about all ranks. Turning a ladder of computational nulls into a rank-unbounded structural obstruction requires an actual argument, and the repo does not have one.

The live, nearer target is deformed Kerr at ranks 3 and 4, named in the repo's own §85 caveat: "a higher-order quartic Killing tensor isn't excluded." A prior-art sweep found nothing excluding rank-3 or rank-4 for Kerr or deformed Kerr, and Cariglia–Galajinsky (arXiv:1503.02162) construct Ricci-flat metrics that carry irreducible rank-3 and rank-4 tensors. The objects exist. There is no theorem saying deformed Kerr cannot have one.

An earlier attempt at this is marked DEAD END in the repo — 7.5 hours, no output, an expand-everything blow-up. The obstruction was the normalizer, not the mathematics. The momentum-space formulation the grading theorem licenses is what made it tractable; the system size is measurable up front rather than discovered by hanging.

The sequence, which is not optional

This is a real dependency order, not a checklist. Step 3 produces a number that is worth nothing without steps 1 and 2, and the repo has already paid for learning that.

  1. Measure the system size and confirm the bracket builds

    Before any physics. The failure mode being avoided is a nine-hour hang that hides a malformed target.

  2. Validate the prover on known-nonzero cases

    Kerr rank 2 must yield Carter — already done, shown above. Cariglia–Galajinsky must yield its rank-3/4 tensor. The control already exists as scripts/_kt_cg5d.py, and it carries a warning worth reading in full: derive the target from their Eq. (24), do not transcribe Eq. (29). PDF-to-text collapsed an index there — K_ttxw read as K_tttw — and the transcribed tensor is not conserved, drift 0.30. A silently wrong target reads as "the control failed" or, far worse, as "no rank-4 KT exists."

    A null from an unvalidated prover is worthless. This ordering is the whole reason the ZV zeros are citable.

Failure modes this program has already paid for

These are not general advice. Each one is an error that was actually made here, cost real time, and is the reason a specific guard exists in the code.

  • A correct computation answering the wrong question. The §124 error. A mis-transcribed metric solves no field equation, and "no Killing tensor survives" on it is guaranteed and meaningless. Verify a transcribed metric is Ricci-flat before calling it a spacetime.
  • A control that cannot fail is not a control. Contributed by tabula, from nine catalogued ways a bug reads as a result. Its companion: a threshold tested in one direction only is not tested.
  • Residuals that vanish exactly where the fit was made are a fit artifact, not a physics failure. This nearly condemned a correct sGB metric — fitting constants as pure numbers at m=1 made every residual proportional to (m⁴ − 1). Solving for c(m) symbolically recovered the dimensional relation instead of discarding it.
  • Proxies masquerade as laws on homogeneous catalogs. The program's flagship — legible ⟺ Killing–Yano integrable, 8/8 agreement, φ = 1.0 — died exactly this way. All eight metrics were found rather than designed, and a 1976 theorem forces the implication on type-D vacua. The measurement was real; the law read off it was not.
  • A relayed claim is repeated, not corroborated. The originating repo must tag it. Attribution drifts to the last speaker unless someone logs the middle hop.

Two alternates, if this one closes or stalls

Is the astrophysical black hole Kerr?

floor measured

All four oracles already meet here and the spine closes: proof, representation-learning and measurement independently give a black hole as a 2-number object, with GW250114 at δ = −0.16. What makes it live is that the program measured its own wall — σ(δ) ∝ 1/SNR is an exact analytic identity, but total error saturates at an SNR-independent waveform-systematic floor. More data cannot cross it; better waveforms can.

And the EdGB field-equation setup was validated four days ago against Ayzenberg & Yunes: fit c₁ = −2m⁴, c₂ = +2m⁴ on the tt component, then require the same constants to reproduce rr, θθ, φφ — all four residuals zero. So beyond-GR corrections can now be derived rather than transcribed. The chain exists end to end: exact O(χ) and O(χ²) sGB corrections → QNM spectrum → ringdown templates → injection into real O4b noise against the measured background → a statement of what deviation would actually be detectable.

Does geometry emerge from entanglement?

observable unstable

Biggest question of the three in physics terms, weakest as a test of a model. Tabula's Phase J already recovered geometry, curvature and an AdS-like radial direction from entanglement alone; quantum measures entanglement corner coefficients, which are genuine CFT observables. The holographic dictionary is the bridge between them.

But last night is a direct warning about attempting it. At matched resolution and with matched kernel specifications, the corner residual's numerator disagrees 2.4× between the square and triangular lattices, and the sign of the zero-mode effect flips between s=1 and s=6. The denominators agree to 2.6% across both geometries; the whole disagreement sits in the numerator. Before this bridges anything, someone has to establish whether the corner coefficient is a well-defined observable in these hands. That is the honest first move and it is not a small one.
